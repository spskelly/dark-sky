# Moon surface

`moon-full.jpg` is NASA Scientific Visualization Studio's full-moon still,
used unchanged. The SVG renderer crops the black margins and masks the lit
portion for each phase, rotating the image 180 degrees for the southern view.

- Source: [Moon Phase and Libration, 2025](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5415/), “Full Moon” in the named phases gallery.
- Original image: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a005000/a005048/phase_full.1571_print.jpg
- Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio. Visualization by Ernie Wright; lunar terrain and imagery from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter.
- Usage: [SVS public-domain policy](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/#can-i-use-your-visualizations) and [NASA media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). No third-party restriction is marked on this still. NASA is credited on the site, preview and exported social card.

This is a fixed surface image with a geometric phase mask. It does not simulate
libration or changing shadows within individual craters.
